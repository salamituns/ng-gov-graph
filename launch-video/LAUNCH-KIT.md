## Launch day

1. **Before posting:** open the site on your own phone and check the first-visit hint, an office, and "Who represents me?" for your state.
2. **Post** on a weekday (Tuesday to Thursday), around 7–9 pm WAT, when Nigerian social traffic peaks. X thread first, then TikTok/Reels, then WhatsApp Status.
3. **First hour:** reply to comments; pin the X thread.
4. **Watch** the [launch dashboard](https://us.posthog.com/project/643765/dashboard/2166227): visitors, where they come from, and the **Feedback inbox**. Fix reported errors fast and say thanks publicly; it builds trust.
5. **Next day:** send the personal notes above, with the video attached.

## Before you post: two things to confirm

- **"Independent / non-partisan":** the site's footer calls it an independent civic project. If you describe it as non-partisan or unsponsored, make sure that's true for you.
- **Pidgin line:** only use it after a native speaker has checked it.
